\subsection{KV-cache and internal-state privacy}
Prior work reconstructs text from embeddings and internal activations~\cite{morris2023,li2023,dong2025}. DeepInvert learns from labeled shadow data and unlabeled obfuscated representations~\cite{deepinvert}; PMA aligns co-occurrence structures from obfuscated streams and public text~\cite{pma}. \scheme{} attacks plaintext caches and proposes reversible obfuscation~\cite{kvcloak}; we target its protected first-layer Value cache.

EarlyBird infers prompts through shared-resource timing~\cite{earlybird}. Our attacker instead observes protected matrices and uses their numerical structure.

\subsection{Attacks on permutation and obfuscation defenses}
Plaintext attacks on permutation-only multimedia ciphers already exploit structure instead of enumerating permutation keys~\cite{permutationciphers}. For language models, Hidden No More recovers prompts from permuted hidden states~\cite{hidden}. Saini et al. recover reused noise subspaces in partial-TEE systems using zero and basis-vector queries~\cite{partialtee}. Collapse aligns multiple refreshed views of obfuscated weights and separates low-rank masks~\cite{collapse}.

ArrowRevelio removes a shared mask direction and matches weight columns to a public ancestor~\cite{arrowrevelio}. SpectralLeak and LatticeLeak target real-valued and modular shared-direction masks~\cite{spectralleak}. BiShield-TEE analyzes unilateral weight obfuscation through mask extraction, alignment, and least-squares recovery~\cite{bishield}.

Our legal repeated text fixes the data term under every permutation, exposing the marker's orbit through unknown dense row mixing. State differences identify an equivalent cache demixer. Table~\ref{tab:neighbors} compares the observed objects, input capabilities, and recovery steps.

\subsection{Ordering recovered content}
Schmaltz et al. order word bags using language-model scoring and beam search~\cite{wordorder}; IBIS searches for high-likelihood permutations under a fixed language model~\cite{ibis}. We apply constrained ordering after cache recovery, retaining block boundaries and reconstructed context, and compare random-order, oracle-bag, and context-ablation conditions.

\subsection{Confidential inference and refreshed mixing}
Confidential inference uses secure computation, trusted execution, and obfuscation~\cite{sok}. GELO refreshes mixing per batch and studies Gram leakage and blind-source separation~\cite{gelo}; ConjFormer supports orthogonally rotated inference~\cite{conjformer}. CryptoGen combines homomorphic encryption and secret sharing for encrypted KV reuse~\cite{cryptogen}; Cachemir develops homomorphic packing and bootstrapping for cached decoding~\cite{cachemir}. VeilKV-Plus protects stored bytes and restores K/V inside its trusted boundary before attention.

Selective sharing also has direct precedents. SafeKV isolates sensitive entries through classification and cache management~\cite{safekv}; CachePrune supports fine-grained reuse of non-sensitive segments~\cite{cacheprune}; PrefixWall detects suspicious cross-user reuse and selectively isolates prefixes~\cite{prefixwall}. Their focus is reuse side channels. Our prototype combines an explicit public-artifact/private-tail policy with authenticated block metadata and prefix-to-tail connection checks. Its byte protection uses established key derivation and encrypt-then-MAC composition~\cite{hkdf,etm}.

\subsection{Positioning relative to the original CPA}
The \scheme{} paper considers chosen-plaintext attacks on simpler linear constructions and adds a marker and per-block permutation~\cite{kvcloak}. Our analysis retains the complete tested transform. Its fresh permutation prevents direct alignment of arbitrary plaintext differences; repeated inputs instead isolate the marker's \emph{post-permutation orbit}, without knowing or equating the sampled permutations.

The official-entry, isolated fused-mode, fresh-secret, and lifecycle experiments in Section~\ref{sec:evaluation} test the resulting demixer and per-block content recovery.
